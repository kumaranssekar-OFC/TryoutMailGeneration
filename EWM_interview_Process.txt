## 2️⃣ Core Architecture Questions (Must Know)

### ⭐ Explain SAP EWM Architecture

**Key Points to Cover:**

*   Embedded vs Decentralized EWM
*   Connection via **RFC / qRFC**
*   Role of **SCM Basis** (in decentralized)
*   Queue monitoring (**SMQ1 / SMQ2**)
*   CIF relevance (only SCM-based EWM)

✅ **Senior Answer Tip:**  
Mention *why embedded EWM is preferred in S/4* (simpler landscape, real-time posting).

***

### ⭐ Difference: WM vs EWM

| WM                 | EWM                            |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| Stock-managed      | **Task & bin-oriented**        |
| Limited automation | **RF, MFS, Yard, Labour Mgmt** |
| Direct posting     | **Asynchronous queue-based**   |
| No slotting        | **Slotting & rearrangement**   |

💡 Highlight scalability & high-volume suitability of EWM.
